County-Level ICE Public-Information Report

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in Southern states, particularly Texas, Florida, Arizona, and California. These states have the highest number of detainees across all demographic groups, with Texas having the largest total population and the highest percentage of non-citizens among all states.

Men 18-34, Non-US Citizen, appear most affected by counts and rates in available data. This age group accounts for a significant portion of ICE detainees, with a notable increase observed over time. The rate of change is particularly high, indicating a growing trend in this demographic.

There are observable changes or trends over time. For instance, the number of detainees has increased steadily since 2025-10-01, with some fluctuations throughout the year. Notably, there was a significant spike in detainees on 2025-12-01, which may be attributed to various factors such as changes in immigration policies or seasonal patterns.
