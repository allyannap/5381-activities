County-Level ICE Public-Information Report:

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in states with large immigrant populations, such as Texas and Florida. The data shows that Latino individuals, who make up a significant portion of the immigrant population, are most affected by counts and rates in available data.

The percentage of non-citizen individuals in ICE facilities varies across states, ranging from 10.7% in Texas to 6.3% in Arizona. The proportion of foreign-born individuals within these facilities also differs, with Florida having the highest percentage at 21.88%, followed by Texas at 17.77%.

Observable changes or trends over time are not explicitly present in this data set. However, it is worth noting that ICE detention patterns have been a topic of concern and scrutiny due to allegations of inhumane treatment and abuse within facilities.
