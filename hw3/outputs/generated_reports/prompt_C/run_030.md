County-Level ICE Public-Information Report

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in Southern states, with the highest counts and rates observed in Texas (TX) and Arizona (AZ). These states have a significant number of ICE facilities and detainees.

The Latino demographic group is most affected by counts and rates in available data. In 2025, Latinos made up approximately 48% of all detainees across the United States. The percentage of Latino detainees has increased over time, with a notable jump observed between September and December of each year.

There are observable changes or trends over time. For example, the number of detainees at Adelanto ICE Processing Center in Los Angeles County, California, has fluctuated significantly since 2025, with an increase from 1410 to 1565 and then back down to 1510. Similarly, the number of Latino detainees at South Texas ICE Complex in Webb County, Texas, has also shown a trend of increasing counts over time.
