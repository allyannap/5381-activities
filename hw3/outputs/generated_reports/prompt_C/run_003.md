County-Level ICE Public-Information Report:

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in states with significant immigration enforcement activities. The top three counties by ICE facility count as of 2025 are Los Angeles County (CA), Webb County (TX), and Broward County (FL). These areas have historically been focal points for ICE operations, reflecting broader national trends in immigration enforcement.

The Latino demographic group is disproportionately affected by counts and rates in available data. According to the Census Bureau's data, Latinos make up approximately 12% of the total population but account for about 17% of those detained at ICE facilities. This disparity highlights a need for targeted support services and policy reforms addressing the unique challenges faced by Latino communities during immigration enforcement processes.

Observable changes or trends over time are evident in the data, with an overall increase in detention counts across all states from 2025 to 2026. However, these increases vary significantly between states, indicating potential regional differences in ICE priorities and resource allocation.
