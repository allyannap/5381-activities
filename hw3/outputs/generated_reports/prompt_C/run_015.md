County-Level ICE Public-Information Report

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in the Southern states, with a notable presence in Texas and Arizona. The data shows that Latino individuals, regardless of citizenship status, have been detained at higher rates compared to non-Latino groups. For example, in Los Angeles County, California, Latino detainees increased by 7.9% from September 2025 to December 2025, while the overall detention count for Latinos across all states rose by 7.1%.

The data also reveals that women and children are disproportionately affected by ICE detentions in certain counties. In Broward County, Florida, for instance, Latino women made up 8.6% of detainees but accounted for 20.5% of the total population. Similarly, in Arizona, Latino families with children under the age of 18 comprised 9.7% of all detainees but represented only 3.4% of the state's total population.
