County-Level ICE Public-Information Report:

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in Southern states, particularly Texas and Arizona. The Adelanto ICE Processing Center in Los Angeles County, California, and the South Texas ICE Complex in Webb County, Texas, serve as major hubs for processing detainees.

The Latino demographic group is disproportionately represented among detainees, with a significant number of non-US citizens held at these facilities. For example, on 2025-12-01, the Adelanto ICE Processing Center in Los Angeles County had a detention count of 1892 for Latino individuals and 1690 for non-US citizens.

The data indicates that women from Caribbean or Latin America are also held at these facilities, with counts ranging from 630 to 725 on 2025-11-01 in Broward County, Florida.
