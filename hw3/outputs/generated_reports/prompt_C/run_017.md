County-Level ICE Public-Information Report

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in the Southern states of Texas, Florida, and Arizona. These states have a higher concentration of ICE facilities compared to other regions, reflecting their significant immigration enforcement activities.

The Latino demographic group is most affected by counts and rates in available data. According to ICE Table 1, Latino individuals account for the majority of detainees across all counties, with a notable increase observed over time. For example, in Los Angeles County, the number of Latino detainees increased from 1510 in September 2025 to 1844 in December 2025, representing an 11% rise.
