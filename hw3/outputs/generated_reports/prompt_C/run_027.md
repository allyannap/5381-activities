ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in Southern states, with the highest counts observed in Texas and Arizona. The data shows that Latino individuals, both U.S. citizens and non-citizens, account for a significant portion of these detainees. Notably, there has been an increase in the number of detainees at facilities like the Adelanto ICE Processing Center in Los Angeles County, California, and the South Texas ICE Complex in Webb County, Texas.
