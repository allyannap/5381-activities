ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in Southern states, with Texas and Arizona having the highest number of facilities. The data shows that Latino individuals, regardless of citizenship status, are detained at a higher rate than non-Latino individuals.

The data indicates that Latino individuals, both citizens and non-citizens, are more likely to be detained compared to their proportion in the total population. For example, in Los Angeles County, California, Latino detainees account for 71% of all detainees despite making up only about 28% of the county's population. Similarly, in Arizona, Latino individuals make up approximately 35% of the state's population but are detained at a rate nearly twice that of their proportion (6.69 vs. 3.40).
