County-Level ICE Public-Information Report

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in Southern states, with Los Angeles County and Webb County in Texas being the most heavily impacted areas. The data shows that Latino individuals, both US citizens and non-citizens, account for a significant portion of ICE detainees across these counties.

The data indicates that Latino individuals, specifically those who are non-US citizens, have been detained at higher rates compared to their overall population in Los Angeles County and Webb County. For instance, in Los Angeles County, the detention count for Latino individuals increased from 1510 in 2025-09 to 1844 in 2025-11, representing a 7.9% increase over this period. Similarly, in Webb County, the detention count for Latino individuals rose from 1690 in 2025-09 to 1892 in 2025-11, showing an 8.7% increase.
