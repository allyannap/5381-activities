ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in Southern states, particularly Texas and Arizona. These two states account for the majority of ICE facility counts (1844 and 73) reported between 2025-12-01 to 2026-09-01.

The Latino demographic group is disproportionately affected by these detention patterns, with a significant increase in counts over time (7.1% from 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-01). Non-US citizens are also more likely to be detained at ICE facilities compared to US citizens (detention count: 1510 vs. 1410, percent change: 7.1%).
