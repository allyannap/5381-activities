County-Level ICE Public-Information Report:

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in Southern states, with Texas and Arizona having the highest number of facilities. The data shows that Latino individuals, who make up a significant portion of detainees, have been impacted by these trends.

The most affected groups appear to be Latino individuals, both non-citizens and citizens, as well as women from Caribbean or Latin America in Broward County, Florida. These demographics are concentrated in facilities such as the Adelanto ICE Processing Center in Los Angeles County, California, and the South Texas ICE Complex in Webb County, Texas.

Observable changes over time include an increase in detention counts at some facilities, particularly those with a high concentration of Latino detainees. However, these trends are not consistently observed across all facilities or states.
