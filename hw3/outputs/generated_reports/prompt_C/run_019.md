County-Level ICE Public-Information Report

Introduction:
ICE (Immigration and Customs Enforcement) detention centers are facilities where non-citizens, primarily undocumented immigrants, are held pending deportation proceedings. This report focuses on the concentration of ICE detention patterns in specific counties across the United States using data from 2025.

County-Level Data:
The provided source context includes two datasets: ICE Table 1 and Census Table A. ICE Table 1 contains synthetic lab data for Adelanto, South Texas, Webb, Broward, and Pinal counties in California, Texas, Florida, and Arizona respectively. The data covers Latino and non-US citizen groups with detention counts, prior values, current values, percent changes, and demographic and citizenship group information.

Census Table A provides state-level context for the same states mentioned in ICE Table 1: California, Texas, Florida, and Arizona. It includes total population, non-citizen population, foreign-born population, below-poverty rate, and ice facility count
