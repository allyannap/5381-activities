County-Level ICE Public-Information Report

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in the Southern states, with Texas and Arizona having the highest number of facilities. The data shows that Latino individuals make up a significant portion of detainees at these facilities, with 30% of all detainees being non-US citizens.

The data indicates that Latino individuals are disproportionately affected by counts and rates in available data. For example, in Los Angeles County, California, the number of Latinos detained increased from 1510 to 1844 over a period of one year (7.9% increase). Similarly, in Broward County, Florida, the number of Latino detainees also increased from 630 to 725 over the same period (10.2% increase).
