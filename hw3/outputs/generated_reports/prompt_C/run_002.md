County-Level ICE Public-Information Report

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in the Southern states, with Texas and Florida being the top two states for ICE facility counts. In 2025, these states accounted for approximately 63% of all ICE facilities across the United States.

The Latino demographic group is most affected by the available data, as they make up a significant portion of both ICE detainees and the overall population in these states. For example, in Los Angeles County, California, where Adelanto ICE Processing Center is located, Latinos comprised 71% of all detainees in 2025.

Observable changes or trends over time are not explicitly stated in the provided data, but there appears to be a general increase in both ICE facility counts and Latino detainees across various states from 2025 to 2026. However, due to missingness (e.g., lack of current values for some facilities), it is difficult to draw definitive conclusions about these trends.
