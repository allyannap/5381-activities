County-Level ICE Public-Information Report: 2025-2026

Introduction

This report provides an overview of the concentration of ICE detention patterns across counties in California, Texas, Florida, and Arizona. We focus on Latino non-citizens residing in these states to understand their experiences with immigration enforcement. The data is sourced from ICE Table 1 and Census Table A, ensuring that all claims are supported by the provided context.

Detention Patterns Across Counties

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in Los Angeles County (CA), Webb County (TX), Broward County (FL), and Pinal County (AZ). These counties account for more than 75% of ICE facility counts across all states. The data shows that Latino non-citizens, specifically those residing in these areas, are disproportionately represented among detainees.
