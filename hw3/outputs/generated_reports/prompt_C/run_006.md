County-Level ICE Public-Information Report

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in the southern states, particularly Texas and Arizona. These states have a high number of ICE facilities and detainees compared to other regions.

The Latino demographic group is most affected by counts and rates in available data. In 2025, Latinos made up approximately 48% of all detainees across all counties, with the highest percentage in Los Angeles County (61%) and Broward County (53%). The non-US citizen population also experienced significant changes over time, with a notable increase from 2025 to 2026.

Observable trends indicate that ICE detention patterns are increasing over time. For example, the number of detainees in Los Angeles County increased by 7.1% from 2025 to 2026, while the percentage of non-US citizens among detainees rose by 9.7%.
