County-Level ICE Public-Information Report

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in the Southern states, particularly Texas and Arizona. The data shows a significant increase in ICE facility counts over time, with notable spikes observed during specific months (e.g., 2025-11-01). Notably, Latino individuals make up a substantial portion of detainees across all facilities, highlighting disparities in the treatment of non-US citizens within these systems.

The data indicates that women and children from Caribbean or Latin American backgrounds are disproportionately represented among detainees at Broward Transitional Center (FL) and Adelanto ICE Processing Center (CA). Additionally, the percentage of foreign-born individuals varies significantly across states, with Texas having a notably higher proportion compared to other regions.

Observable trends in available data suggest an increase in ICE facility counts over time, particularly during specific months. However, due to missingness and denominator limitations, it is difficult to draw definitive conclusions about the long-term implications of these trends.
