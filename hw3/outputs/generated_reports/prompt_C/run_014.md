County-Level ICE Public-Information Report

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in the southern states, particularly Texas and Arizona. The Adelanto ICE Processing Center in Los Angeles County, California, and the South Texas ICE Complex in Webb County, Texas, have been among the busiest facilities. These locations account for a significant portion of ICE detainees across all years analyzed.

The Latino demographic group is disproportionately represented at these facilities, with non-US citizens making up a substantial percentage of detainees. The Adelanto ICE Processing Center saw an increase in both detention counts and current values from 2025 to 2026, indicating higher occupancy rates during this period.

Women, particularly those aged 18-34, are also represented at these facilities, with the Eloy Detention Center in Arizona being one of the primary centers for women's detention. The percentage of foreign-born individuals within these facilities has remained relatively stable over time, ranging from 9% to 12%.
