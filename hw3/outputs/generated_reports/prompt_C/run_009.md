County-Level ICE Public-Information Report:

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in the Southern states, particularly Texas and Arizona. The Adelanto ICE Processing Center in Los Angeles County, California, and the South Texas ICE Complex in Webb County, Texas, serve as major hubs for processing detainees.

The Latino demographic group is disproportionately represented among detainees, with a significant presence at these facilities. Non-US citizens are more likely to be detained than US citizens, reflecting broader trends of immigration enforcement.

Observable changes or trends over time in available data include an increase in detention counts and rates for certain demographics, such as Latino non-citizens and women from Caribbean/Latin America. However, these trends may not be comprehensive due to missingness and denominator limitations in the source context.

Caveats and missingness limits should be disclosed regarding the availability of data on specific demographics, facilities, and time periods.
