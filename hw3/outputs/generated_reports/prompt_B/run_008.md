ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
Counties with the highest ICE detention counts are primarily located along the southern border, including Los Angeles County in California, Webb County in Texas, and Broward County in Florida. These areas have seen significant growth in ICE facilities over recent years.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
The data indicates that Latino individuals, particularly those who are non-citizens, appear to be disproportionately affected by the observed counts and rates of ICE detention. This is evident from the high numbers of detainees at facilities in Los Angeles County, Webb County, and Broward County.

Observable Changes or Trends Over Time:
There appears to be a steady increase in both the number of detainees and the rate of change over time. For example, between 2025-10-01 and 2025-12-01, Los Angeles County saw an increase from 1410 to 1892 detainees, a 37% rise in just one month.
