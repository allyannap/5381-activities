ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The data shows that ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in states with large Hispanic populations, such as California (Los Angeles County), Texas (South Texas ICE Complex and Webb County), Florida (Broward County), Arizona (Pinal County), and New York. These areas have seen significant growth in ICE facility counts over the past year, indicating a concentration of detention centers in these regions.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
The data suggests that Latino individuals are most affected by counts and rates in available data. This is evident from the high numbers of detainees at facilities such as Adelanto ICE Processing Center (Los Angeles County) and South Texas ICE Complex (Texas). Additionally, women from Caribbean or Latin America appear to be disproportionately represented among detainees at Broward Transitional Center (Florida), with a significant increase in counts over time.
