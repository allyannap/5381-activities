ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
Counties with the highest ICE detention counts are primarily located along the US-Mexico border, particularly in California and Texas. Los Angeles County, CA, has seen significant increases in detainment rates for Latino individuals, while Webb County, TX, has experienced notable growth for non-US citizens from various ethnic backgrounds.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
The data suggests that Latino individuals are disproportionately represented among ICE detainees across all states. This is evident from the high counts of Latino detainees in Los Angeles County, CA, and Webb County, TX. Additionally, non-US citizens from Caribbean/Latin America appear to be overrepresented at certain facilities, such as Eloy Detention Center in Arizona.

Observable Changes or Trends Over Time:
There is a noticeable increase in ICE detention counts across all states since the beginning of 2025. This trend appears to be consistent with broader immigration policies and enforcement efforts.
