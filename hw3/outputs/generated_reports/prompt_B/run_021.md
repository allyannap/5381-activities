ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
Counties with the highest ICE detention counts are primarily located on the West Coast, particularly in California and Texas. Los Angeles County (CA) has the highest number of detainees at Adelanto ICE Processing Center, while Webb County (TX) has the highest count at South Texas ICE Complex.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
The data indicates that Latino individuals are overrepresented in ICE detention centers across various states. Non-US citizens, specifically those from Caribbean or Latin America, are also disproportionately represented in these facilities. Women, particularly those aged 18-34, are another group with a notable presence at the Broward Transitional Center in Florida.
