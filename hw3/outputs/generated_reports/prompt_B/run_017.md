ICE detention patterns are concentrated in states with large immigrant populations, such as Texas and California. The data shows that Latino individuals, who make up a significant portion of the immigrant population, are most affected by ICE detention counts and rates. For example, in Los Angeles County, the Adelanto ICE Processing Center saw an increase in detainment counts from 1410 to 1565 between September 2025 and October 2025, a 7.9% rise. Similarly, in Webb County, Texas, the South Texas ICE Complex experienced a 9.1% increase in detainment counts from November 2025 to December 2025.
