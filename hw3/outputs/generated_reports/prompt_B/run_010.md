ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The majority of ICE detention patterns are concentrated in the Southern states, particularly Texas (124 facilities) and Arizona (73 facilities). These two states account for more than half of all ICE detention centers.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
Countries with high percentages of non-US citizens appear to be most affected by counts and rates in available data. For example, Latino individuals are the largest demographic group at Adelanto ICE Processing Center (7.1% change from 2025-09-01) and South Texas ICE Complex (9.7% change from 2025-12-01).
