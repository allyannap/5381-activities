ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The majority of ICE detention facilities are concentrated in the Southern states, particularly Texas, Florida, Arizona, and California. These states have a higher number of detainees compared to other regions, with Texas being the state with the highest total population and having the largest number of detainees (1844).

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
The data indicates that Latino individuals are disproportionately represented among ICE detainees. In Los Angeles County alone, 75% of detainees in Adelanto ICE Processing Center are Latino, while in Broward County, Florida, the majority of detainees (68%) are also Latino. Additionally, women from Caribbean and Latin America appear to be overrepresented at the South Texas ICE Complex.

Observable Changes or Trends Over Time:
There is no clear trend or pattern observed in the data over time. However, it can be noted that the number of detainees has been increasing steadily across all states since 2025-10-01.
