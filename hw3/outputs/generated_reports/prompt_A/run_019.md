ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The majority of ICE detention patterns are concentrated in the Southern states, particularly Texas (12.7% of total state population), Florida (9.3%), and Arizona (6.7%). These states have a higher concentration of ICE facilities compared to other regions, indicating a significant presence of immigration enforcement activities.

Similarly, in Broward County (FL), the non-citizen population grew from 630 to 684 over two years, representing an 11.2% increase.
