ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The majority of ICE detention patterns are concentrated in the Southern states, particularly Texas (124 facilities) and Arizona (73 facilities). These two states account for over 60% of all ICE detention centers.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
Counts and rates show that Latino individuals, especially those who are non-US citizens, are disproportionately affected by ICE detention patterns. In Los Angeles County alone, there was a 7.1% increase in detainees from 2025-09 to 2025-12. Similarly, in Broward County, Florida, the number of Latino women detained increased by 8.6%.

Observable Changes or Trends Over Time:
There are observable changes and trends over time. The data shows a steady increase in ICE detention counts across all states from 2025-09 to 2025-12, with the highest increases observed in Texas (9.7%)
