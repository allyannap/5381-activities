ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The majority of ICE detention patterns are concentrated in the Southern states, particularly Texas (10.7% of total state population), Florida (9.3%), and Arizona (6.7%). These states have a higher percentage of non-citizen populations and more ICE facilities compared to other regions.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
The data shows that Latino individuals, particularly those who are Non-US Citizens, appear most affected by counts and rates in available data. The detention count for Latino individuals is consistently higher than non-Latino individuals across all states and facilities.
