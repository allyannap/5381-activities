ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated: 

The ICE detention centers are concentrated primarily in the southern states of Texas, Arizona, and Florida. These states have a higher number of detainees compared to other regions, with Texas having the highest count at 1892 on December 31, 2025.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates: 

The data suggests that Latino individuals, particularly those who are non-US citizens, are most affected by ICE detention patterns. The facilities in Los Angeles County, California (Adelanto ICE Processing Center), Webb County, Texas (South Texas ICE Complex), and Broward County, Florida (Broward Transitional Center) have the highest counts of detainees among all counties.

Observable Changes or Trends Over Time: 

There are no clear observable changes in detention patterns over time based on the provided data. However, there is a noticeable increase in the number of detainees at certain facilities and states during specific months (e.g., December 2025).
