ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The ICE detention facilities are primarily concentrated in the Southern states of Texas, Florida, Arizona, and California. These states account for a significant majority of the total ICE facility count across all years.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates?
The data indicates that Latino individuals, particularly those who are non-US citizens, appear to be most affected by counts and rates in available data. This is evident from the high detention counts for these groups at various facilities and over time. Additionally, women of Caribbean or Latin American descent also show a notable trend with increasing numbers of detainees.

Observable Changes or Trends Over Time:
There are observable changes in ICE detention patterns over time. For example, the number of detainees has consistently increased from 2025 to 2026 for both Latino and non-US citizen groups at various facilities. Furthermore, there is a noticeable increase in the percentage of detainees among the foreign born population across all states.
