County-Level ICE Public-Information Report Summary:

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in Southern states, particularly Texas and Arizona. The data shows that these states have the highest number of detainees across various demographic groups, including Latino non-citizens, women from Caribbean or Latin America, and men aged 18-34.

The data indicates that Latino non-citizens are disproportionately represented among ICE detainees, with a significant increase in their count over time (7.1 to 9.7 percent change). Women from Caribbean or Latin America also have a notable presence at the Eloy Detention Center and Broward Transitional Center, with counts increasing by 8.6 and 10.2 percent respectively.

Notably, Texas has the highest number of ICE detainees across all demographic groups, with a total count of 1844 on December 31st, 2025. This concentration is likely due to the state's significant presence of ICE facilities and its role as a major entry point for migrants from Latin America
