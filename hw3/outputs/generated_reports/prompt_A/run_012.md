ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The majority of ICE detention patterns are concentrated in the Southern states, particularly Texas (124 facilities) and Arizona (73 facilities). California has 124 facilities, while Florida has 54 facilities.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
Women, specifically those from Caribbean or Latin America, appear to be most affected by counts and rates in available data. In Los Angeles County, for instance, the Broward Transitional Center houses women from these regions, with a notable increase in detainees (8.6% change) over the course of 2025.
