ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The majority of ICE detention patterns are concentrated in the Southern states, particularly Texas (124 facilities) and Arizona (73 facilities). California has 124 facilities as well. Florida also has a significant presence with 54 facilities.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
The data indicates that Latino individuals are disproportionately affected by ICE detention patterns, particularly those who are non-US citizens (detention count rates of 7.1 to 9.7 per facility). Women from Caribbean or Latin America also appear in high numbers at facilities like the Broward Transitional Center and South Texas ICE Complex.

Observable Changes or Trends Over Time:
There is no clear trend visible over time, as data only spans a single year (2025) for each facility. However, it can be observed that some states have higher numbers of facilities than others, suggesting potential regional differences in ICE operations and detention patterns.
