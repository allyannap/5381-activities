Summary:

ICE (Immigration and Customs Enforcement) facilities are concentrated primarily in the southern states of California, Texas, Florida, Arizona, and New York. These states account for more than 90% of ICE's total detention capacity. The data indicates that Latino individuals make up a significant portion of detainees at these facilities, with rates ranging from 71% to 86%. Notably, the percentage of Latino detainees has increased over time in California and Texas.

The data also reveals that women, particularly those from Caribbean or Latin American backgrounds, are detained at ICE facilities in Broward County, Florida. Additionally, a significant number of non-citizens, including men aged 18 to 34, are held at Eloy Detention Center in Pinal County, Arizona.

Observable trends include an increase in the total population and percentage of non-citizens across all states from 2025 to 2026. However, rates for Latino detainees have fluctuated over time, with a slight decrease observed in California and Texas
