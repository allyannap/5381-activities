ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The majority of ICE detention patterns are concentrated in the Southern states, particularly Texas (12.70% of total state population), Florida (9.56%), and Arizona (6.69%). California has a significant presence as well, with 12.32% of its population being non-citizens.

The data indicates that Latino individuals are disproportionately affected by ICE detention patterns.
