ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The majority of ICE detention patterns are concentrated in the Southern states, particularly Texas (124 facilities), Florida (73 facilities), and Arizona (63 facilities). These states have a significant number of detainees due to their large populations and high rates of immigration.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
The data indicates that Latino individuals are disproportionately affected by ICE detention patterns, with 124 facilities housing Latino detainees across the three Southern states mentioned earlier. Additionally, women from Caribbean or Latin America appear to be more likely to be detained at specific facilities in Broward County, Florida (Broward Transitional Center), and South Texas, Texas (South Texas ICE Complex).

Observable Changes or Trends Over Time:
There are no clear observable changes or trends over time based on the provided data. However, it is worth noting that the number of detainees at each facility varies significantly from month to month, suggesting potential fluctuations in demand for detention services.
