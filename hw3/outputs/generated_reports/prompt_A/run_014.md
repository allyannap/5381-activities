ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:

The county-level ICE public-information reports reveal that the majority of ICE detention facilities are concentrated in Southern states, particularly Texas and Arizona. These two states account for a significant portion of all ICE detainees across the United States.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates in Available Data:

The data indicates that Latino individuals, specifically those who are non-US citizens, appear to be most affected by counts and rates in available data. This is evident from the high detention counts at facilities like Adelanto ICE Processing Center in Los Angeles County, California, and South Texas ICE Complex in Webb County, Texas.

Observable Changes or Trends Over Time:

There are no clear observable changes or trends over time based on the available data. However, it is worth noting that there has been a steady increase in detention counts at facilities across all states since 2025.
