ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
Counties with the highest ICE detention counts are primarily located in Southern states, such as Texas (Webb County), Arizona (Pinal County), and Florida (Broward County). These areas have a higher concentration of ICE facilities processing non-US citizens.

Who Appears Most Affected by Counts and Rates:
The data indicates that Latino individuals are disproportionately represented in the ICE detention population, particularly those who are Non-US Citizens. Women from Caribbean or Latin American backgrounds also appear to be overrepresented in certain facilities.

Observable Changes or Trends Over Time:
There is a noticeable increase in ICE facility counts and detainment numbers across various states, especially during the winter months (December to February). This trend suggests that the number of individuals being detained by ICE has been rising over time.

Scale/Rate Caveats or Missingness Limits:
The data does not provide information on the total population in each county, making it difficult to accurately assess the scale and rate of ICE detentions.
