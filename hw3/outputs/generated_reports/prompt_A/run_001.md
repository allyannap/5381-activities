County-Level ICE Public-Information Report Summary:

ICE detention patterns are concentrated primarily in the Southern states, particularly Texas and Arizona. These two states account for a significant portion of the total ICE facility count across all counties. In 2025, Texas had the highest number of ICE facilities with 1892 detainees, followed by Arizona with 1695 detainees.

The Latino demographic group is most affected by these counts and rates in available data. The data shows that Latino individuals are detained at a higher rate than non-Latino individuals across all states. For example, in Los Angeles County, the detention count for Latino individuals increased from 1410 in 2025-09 to 1565 in 2025-10 and then to 1844 in 2025-11.
