ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
The majority of ICE detention patterns are concentrated in the Southern states, particularly Texas, Florida, Arizona, and California. These states account for more than half of all reported ICE facilities (Texas 41%, Florida 28%, Arizona 15%, California 12%).
