ICE Detention Patterns Concentrated:
Counties with the highest ICE detention counts are primarily located along the southern border of California, Texas, and Arizona. Los Angeles County in California has the highest number of detainment cases (1565), followed by Webb County in Texas (1844) and Pinal County in Arizona (1695).

Who Appears Most Affected:
The data indicates that Latino individuals are disproportionately represented among ICE detainees. In Los Angeles County, 73% of the detainment cases involve non-US citizens, while in Webb County, it is 80%. Similarly, in Arizona, 69% of the detainment cases involve non-US citizens.
